The Firm

Profile

Design That’s Meant to Be Built

We are a residential design studio focused on creating thoughtful, functional, and distinctive spaces that are designed with real-world construction in mind.

From new homes and additions to renovations, interior updates, and detailed construction drawings, we work with homeowners to turn ideas into well-designed, buildable projects. Our approach brings together architectural design, technical knowledge, and hands-on construction experience, allowing us to look at a project from both sides of the process.

We believe good design should be more than something that looks great on paper. It should work for the way you live, make sense to build, and stand the test of time. Whether you are starting from a blank slate or reimagining an existing home, we work closely with each client to develop a design that feels intentional, personal, and uniquely theirs.

At the heart of what we do is a simple idea: design it well, build it right, and make it yours.

Our story

After years of studying architecture and gaining hands-on experience in the construction industry, I wanted to create something of my own. Starting my own practice gives me the opportunity to work directly with clients, take a more personal approach to design, and have greater creative freedom throughout the process.

I believe the best projects come from collaboration, attention to detail, and understanding how a design will actually be built. Working for myself allows me to bring all of those things together while creating homes that are thoughtful, personal, and built around the people who live in them.

How we work

Every project starts with a conversation. We take the time to understand how you live, what you need, what you envision, and what makes your home unique. From there, we translate those ideas into a thoughtful design that balances aesthetics, functionality, and the realities of construction.

We work collaboratively throughout the process, developing the design with you rather than simply designing for you. From the first sketches and concepts through detailed drawings, material selections, and construction, our goal is to make the process clear, creative, and enjoyable.
