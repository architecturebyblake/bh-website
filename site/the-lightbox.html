The Lightbox

About the project

The Lightbox was conceived as a lasting retreat, designed to evolve alongside its owners for years to come. With space for a close-knit family and their animals, the home balances comfort with thoughtful functionality. A dedicated sunroom anchors the design, positioned to capture maximum daylight and bring the owners’ love of gardening and nature into the heart of the home.

A bluestone path leads to a welcoming façade of muted green-gray siding, stone accents, and a dark roof. Black-framed windows wrap the sunroom and living areas, minimizing visual barriers and drawing the surrounding landscape into the home.
