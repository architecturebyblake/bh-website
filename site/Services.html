The Firm

Services

Residential design that is meant to be built. From the first conversation through construction drawings, every project is approached from both the design and the feasibility side.

01

New Homes

Custom residential design from first sketch to permit-ready drawings, shaped around your site, your budget, and the way you live.

02

Additions

Thoughtful expansions that feel original to the house, with attention to structure, roofline, materials, and how new space connects to old.

03

Interior Renovations

Reworking existing homes for better flow, light, and function, with clear plans that respect what is already there.

04

Interior Updates

Kitchens, baths, and living spaces designed with layout, finishes, and built-in details that are practical to build.

05

Construction Drawings

Detailed, coordinated drawings that contractors can price and build from with confidence, informed by hands-on construction experience.

06

Illustrative Rendering

Realistic exterior and interior renderings that help you see the finished design, and make confident decisions on materials, color, and massing before construction begins.
